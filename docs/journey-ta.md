## أوصاف المشاهد (تُرسل بالإنجليزية)

**1. حماة — النواعير**
> A riverside path along the Orontes river in Hama, Syria. Two giant ancient wooden water wheels (norias), about 20 meters tall, made of dark weathered timber with many wooden paddles and boxes, turning slowly and splashing water. Behind each wheel a long stone aqueduct of pale limestone with a row of pointed arches carries the water away from the top of the wheel. Calm green river water, reeds, tall poplar and eucalyptus trees on the banks, old pale stone houses in the far background. Animals: white egrets on the river edge, a family of ducks swimming.

**2. حمص — جامع خالد بن الوليد وساعة حمص**
> A wide stone-paved square in Homs, Syria. In the foreground-right a tall slender square clock tower of light cream stone with a clock face on each side near its top and a small pointed cap (the New Clock Tower of Homs). Behind it, in the middle distance, the Khalid ibn al-Walid Mosque seen from outside: Ottoman-style building with walls in alternating horizontal stripes of black basalt and white limestone (ablaq), one large central lead-grey dome surrounded by smaller domes, and two very tall slender pencil-shaped Ottoman minarets with pointed grey caps and balconies. Garden with palm trees and green lawns around the mosque. Animals: a flock of pigeons on the paving, a cat sitting on a low wall.
> ملاحظة دقّة: الساعة والجامع في الواقع بينهما نحو كيلومتر؛ الصورة تجمعهما بالمنظور (الساعة قريبة والجامع خلفها).

**3. دير الزور — الجسر المعلّق قبل تدميره (2013)**
> The old pedestrian suspension bridge of Deir ez-Zor, Syria, as it was before 2013, crossing the wide Euphrates river. Long and narrow deck of light-painted steel lattice girders, hanging from thick steel suspension cables that sweep down between tall square pylons of pale yellow stone, each pylon topped with an arch. Several spans across the river. Wide calm brownish-green Euphrates water, sandy banks with date palms and reeds, flat desert horizon. The child stands at the start of the bridge deck looking along it. Animals: a grey heron on the bank, swallows flying over the water.
> ⚠️ أحتاج صورة مرجعية حقيقية للجسر قبل التدمير لتأكيد شكل الأبراج وعدد الفتحات قبل التوليد.

**4. السويداء — آثار قنوات (حجر بازلتي أسود)**
> Ancient Roman ruins of Qanawat in the Jabal al-Arab mountains, Suwayda, Syria. Everything built of dark black-grey basalt stone: a row of tall slender basalt columns with carved Corinthian capitals standing in a grove of oak trees, a large carved basalt doorway with a decorated lintel, scattered fallen basalt blocks. Green grass and wildflowers between the black stones, hilly landscape, warm sunlight. Animals: a small flock of goats, a hoopoe bird on a stone.

**5. درعا — مسرح بُصرى الروماني**
> Inside the Roman theatre of Bosra, Daraa, Syria — one of the best preserved Roman theatres in the world. The child stands on the stage floor, back to camera, facing the huge semicircle of steep seating tiers built entirely of dark black basalt stone, rising high, with an arched colonnade gallery at the top. Behind the child, at the edges of the frame, parts of the stage wall with standing columns. Clear blue sky. Animals: two white storks on the top of the seats, a lizard on a basalt step.

**6. طبريا — البحيرة من مرتفعات الجولان**
> High viewpoint on the Golan Heights looking down over the Sea of Galilee (Lake Tiberias). The child stands on a grassy cliff edge path with scattered black basalt boulders. Below, the large calm blue lake fills the middle of the frame, with steep green slopes leading down to it and soft hills on the far western shore in light haze; no clear buildings. Spring green grass and red anemone flowers. Animals: two mountain gazelles on the slope, an eagle soaring above the lake.
